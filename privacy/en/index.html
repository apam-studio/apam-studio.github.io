Privacy Policy – Roll and Score: Cube Adventure

Effective date: 29 October 2026
Policy version: 1.1

This policy explains in plain language what data the Android game Roll and Score: Cube Adventure ("the game") collects, why, who receives it, and how you can delete it.

The short version

  • The game never asks for your name, email, phone number or photos. You can play without signing in.
  • If you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is stored in Google Firebase in Poland (Warsaw) so it comes back when you change phones. The game never receives your email address. Without signing in, everything stays on your phone.
  • The game does not use location (no GPS, no location permission). You pick the country flag in your Avatar yourself, or leave it as "no flag".
  • The game does not collect usage statistics (we do not use Google Analytics or similar tools).
  • To run the game we use Google services: Firebase (sign-in, cloud save and the online leaderboard), AdMob ads and Google Play payments.
  • You appear in the online leaderboard only if you enter a nickname in your Avatar and sign in to Google Play Games. Other players then see your nickname, flag and scores.
  • The game is free and shows ads, but never during a level, after a loss or at start-up. A one-time "No ads" purchase turns off full-screen ads.
  • You can delete your leaderboard entry at any time with "Delete my leaderboard data" in Options, and everything including the cloud copy with "Delete my data". You can also write to nowak.pk@gmail.com.

2.3 Cloud save

Cloud save is optional. You sign in with Google Play Games; you can sign out in Options or never sign in – then your progress stays on the phone only.

  • The game signs you in with your Google Play Games profile. It never receives your email address or password.
  • The cloud holds only what the game already keeps on your phone (section 2.1). Other players can't see the copy; only your account (database rules) and the administrator via the Firebase console can access it.
  • If you play on two phones, the game merges your progress: the best scores and all earned trophies are kept.

2.4 Online leaderboard

The leaderboard is optional. You are in it only if you enter a nickname in your Avatar and sign in to Google Play Games (both steps are up to you). Without a nickname or without signing in, nothing goes to the leaderboard. If you remove your nickname, the game deletes your leaderboard entries.

  • Tables: Adventure, Daily Challenge, each world separately and Hall of Fame (points from all won games). Each table has a worldwide and a country view (by flag).
  • Your nickname is visible to other players. Don't use your real name or contact details. The game rejects nicknames with profanity and ones that look like an email, phone number or web address.
  • Your flag is shown next to your nickname. If you choose "no flag", your score appears only in the worldwide view.
  • "Report nickname" immediately hides that nickname for the reporter and sends us a report. We review reports by hand and hide nicknames that break the rules for everyone.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing). Write to nowak.pk@gmail.com.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, https://uodo.gov.pl) or the supervisory authority in your country.

Ad consent: the consent form (Google UMP) appears in the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland before the first ad. You can change your choice in Options › Privacy › Ad consent.

6. Children and teenagers

The game is not directed at children under 13. On first launch we ask a simple age question to choose the kind of ads. The answer stays on the phone. If a player is under 16 (the GDPR Art. 8 age in Poland is 16), we don't show them personalised ads and don't pass on the advertising ID. The online leaderboard (section 2.4) shows only the nickname, flag and scores – so please don't put your real name in your nickname.

If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. Parents can write to nowak.pk@gmail.com.

7. How to delete your data

In the game: Options › Privacy › "Delete my leaderboard data". After you confirm, the game immediately deletes your leaderboard entry (all tables, including the Daily Challenge). Your progress on the phone and the cloud copy stay. Removing your nickname in the Avatar also deletes your leaderboard entries.

Everything at once: Options › Privacy › "Delete my data" deletes your leaderboard entries, the cloud progress copy and your Firebase account linked to Google Play Games, and signs the game out of cloud save. "Sign out" alone stops syncing but does not delete the copy. You can also remove the game's connection in the Google Play Games app.

Without the game (e.g. after uninstalling): email nowak.pk@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data – Roll and Score". Include your player code (8 characters, shown in Options in the cloud save part) or – if you don't have it – your nickname, flag and the approximate date you last played. We will delete your leaderboard entry, cloud progress copy and account within 30 days and confirm by email. You don't need to reinstall the game. If you don't write, your leaderboard entry disappears anyway after 12 months of inactivity, and the cloud copy after 24 months.

Data on the phone: uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

"No ads" purchase: Google Play keeps the purchase history in your Google account (needed to restore the purchase); you manage it in Google Play.

We may keep minimal data where the law or protection against abuse requires it (e.g. a note about a blocked offensive nickname).

8. Security

Data is sent only over encrypted connections (TLS). Only signed-in players can read the leaderboard, and only the owner of an entry can write it (database rules). The rules also reject scores higher than possible in the game. Only the administrator can hide a reported nickname. Only the signed-in owner can read and change their cloud progress copy. Only the administrator has access to the Firebase console, protected by two-step verification.
